# Current Learning and Research Direction

This page records what I am actively learning. It should not be read as a claim that I have mastered every subject listed here.

## BSc and Academic Direction

I am a BSc Computer Science student with roughly two years remaining. Before graduating, I want to:

1. Build projects that I genuinely understand and can explain.
2. Become able to design, implement, debug, secure, deploy, and maintain systems myself.
3. Aim to publish two or three papers so I can develop real research ability.
4. Strengthen my mathematical foundation for graduate study, AI, image processing, security, and research.
5. Prepare confidently for a master's degree abroad.

The United States and other Western destinations, including Europe, are possibilities. Studying at MIT is a serious aspiration because I admire its rigor, innovation, teaching, and commitment to open education. My present institution and location are intentionally omitted from this public profile.
