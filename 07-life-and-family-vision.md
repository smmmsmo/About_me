# Life and Family Vision

This page describes a future I hope to build. It is an aspiration, not a claim about my present family status.

## What Stability Means to Me

A stable life is not only a salary or job title. I imagine having meaningful engineering work, a wife and family, personal systems that I understand, and enough control over my time to be present for the people I love.

I may work professionally as a software engineer or in another engineering-related role, but I also expect to maintain personal servers, services, automations, and projects outside my job.

## Technology Should Protect Time

My ideal personal infrastructure would monitor itself through automation and agents. If something needs attention, it should notify me clearly on my phone. Otherwise, it should work quietly in the background.

I do not want to spend every free day staring at dashboards. I want to build systems capable enough to protect my time, then give that protected time to my family.

## A Present Husband and Father

On a free day, I would rather be with my wife and children than remain unnecessarily attached to work.

I imagine:

- playing with my children;
- taking them to parks and picnics;
- visiting different places together;
- being physically and emotionally present;
- creating memories in which their father participated;
- supporting my wife's wishes and happiness; and
- visiting my parents and her parents.

I am not naturally someone with many travel wishes. However, if my wife or family wants to go somewhere, I would genuinely try to make that experience possible.

## Passing a Blessing Forward

My own childhood was happy. I had friends I could look up to, enjoyed my life, and remain in contact with those friends. I consider myself blessed.

I do not dream of giving my children a good childhood because mine was missing. I want to pass forward the happiness and companionship that I received.

My hope is simple: my children should remember a father who played with them, explored with them, listened to them, and had a positive presence in their lives. Their smiles and sense of safety would matter more to me than creating a perfect public image of a family.

## The Balance I Want

I want a life in which:

- engineering provides competence and stability;
- automation reduces unnecessary interruption;
- learning continues throughout my life;
- work remains meaningful without consuming everything;
- family receives my real attention; and
- the people I love experience me as present, not merely responsible from a distance.
